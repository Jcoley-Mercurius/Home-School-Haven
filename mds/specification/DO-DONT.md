# Home School Haven MDS Do / Don't Guidance

**System:** Mercurius Design System  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved and locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Identity and tone

### Do

- Preserve the supplied handwritten “Homeschool” wordmark, “Haven of SWFL” descriptor, house line art, and coral heart.
- Place the canonical logo on compatible warm ivory, warm white, or white surfaces.
- Use calm, supportive, relationship-centered language.
- Let the heart-and-home character influence the wider system subtly.

### Don't

- Do not generatively redraw, reinterpret, or replace the canonical logo.
- Do not invent reversed, monochrome, badge, or icon-only logo variants without approval.
- Do not use the handwritten logo lettering as an interface font.
- Do not make the experience childish, cartoon-heavy, overly rustic, or institutionally sterile.

## Color and typography

### Do

- Use Forest 600 for principal actions and structural emphasis.
- Use Coral 500 and Gold 500 as restrained decorative accents.
- Use Coral 700 and Gold 700 when those hues must carry readable meaning.
- Use Lora for editorial display and Manrope for body, controls, navigation, tables, and dashboards.
- Maintain the approved responsive type roles rather than uniformly shrinking desktop text.

### Don't

- Do not use Logo Coral `#ED7D7C` or Heritage Gold `#B38A42` for normal-size text on light surfaces.
- Do not introduce unrelated bright colors, cold grays, neon accents, or pure-black page fields.
- Do not set long operational passages in Lora.
- Do not hardcode a new visual value when an approved token exists.

## Layout and components

### Do

- Use calm whitespace and strong content hierarchy.
- Follow the 12/8/4-column grid and approved container widths.
- Transform layouts intentionally at breakpoints; preserve priority and meaning.
- Reuse, compose, and extend approved components before creating a new one.
- Maintain 44×44 px minimum touch targets and visible keyboard focus.
- Transform operational tables into labeled record cards when narrow widths cannot preserve column integrity.

### Don't

- Do not shrink desktop compositions until they technically fit.
- Do not compress help, validation, consent, payment, or privacy language.
- Do not overuse cards, borders, shadows, pills, or accent colors.
- Do not hide important actions or states merely to simplify mobile layouts.
- Do not create a new reusable component convention without treating it as an MDS gap.

## Trust states

### Do

- Pair every meaningful status color with an icon and explicit label.
- Say when checkout leaves the platform.
- Treat payment return as pending verification until an authoritative result exists.
- State that enrollment is not confirmed when it is not confirmed.
- Present assistance requests privately, respectfully, and without promising outcomes.
- Use owner-approved policy content for consent.

### Don't

- Do not represent an external checkout handoff as successful payment.
- Do not equate payment activity with confirmed enrollment.
- Do not invent capacity, price, schedule, grade, scholarship, discount, refund, or policy facts.
- Do not imply consent when approved consent language or acceptance evidence is unavailable.
- Do not expose unnecessary student or family data.

## Faith and imagery

### Do

- Express faith through character, service, values, encouragement, and authentic community moments.
- Use warm, natural, candid learning imagery with appropriate permissions.
- Write useful alternative text for meaningful imagery and empty alternatives for decoration.

### Don't

- Do not substitute repetitive religious decoration for authentic values.
- Do not imply generated people are real students, families, or staff.
- Do not use imagery that feels like generic corporate stock, exaggerated classroom performance, or juvenile clip art.

## Release boundaries

- The educator Course Builder reference is an approved **future-platform visual direction**, not Foundation Release behavior.
- Foundation Release educator access is limited to assigned programs, schedules, rosters, announcements, and learning resources as authorized by MPS.
- Private beta references and testing may use sample or sanitized data only until real-family policy gaps are resolved.
