# Home School Haven Product Blueprint

**MPS:** v1.0  
**Release:** Foundation Release Beta  
**Decision owner:** Samantha Dodson

Home School Haven is one connected Christ-centered ecosystem combining a public website, program-management portal, blended-learning hub, and future online-learning platform. It serves homeschool families in Cape Coral and Southwest Florida today while preparing for future live and self-paced remote learning.

The Foundation Release is a private owner-review build. It must demonstrate that families can understand programs, choose guidance or direct registration, create a parent-controlled family account, manage student profiles, and see trusted enrollment, schedule, announcement, and resource information. It must demonstrate that educators can operate within assigned programs and that administrators can manage programs, schedules, enrollment, assignments, rosters, and publication states centrally.

Core values are Christ-centered purpose, stewardship, relationship, character over performance, and community over competition. Convenience or growth must not weaken child safety, parental authority, family privacy, or community trust.

The beta uses current approved website content and program-specific external checkout links. It uses sample or sanitized family/student data. It does not automate unresolved policy or treat checkout initiation as payment success. Full Course Builder and launch-scale LMS behavior remain deferred.

The canonical detail and traceability source is `MPS-PROJECT-STATE.yaml`.
