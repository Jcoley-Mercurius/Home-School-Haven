## Approved product truth

Home School Haven is one connected Christ-centered ecosystem: a public website, program-management portal, blended-learning hub, and future online-learning platform for Cape Coral and Southwest Florida.

Foundation Release provides public discovery, inquiry and assistance paths, parent-controlled family accounts and student profiles, program registration with external checkout handoff, family dashboards, assigned-program educator workspaces, administrator operations, announcements, and learning resources.

Parents control student profiles. Students do not receive independent beta accounts. Educators are limited to assigned programs. Administrators control program publication, pricing presentation, registration state, capacity, waitlists, assignments, enrollment operations, and cancellations within approved policy. Samantha retains final authority.

## Hard boundaries

- Use current approved website content and preserve program-specific checkout URLs.
- Checkout initiation is not payment success or confirmed enrollment.
- Use sample or sanitized child/family data for the private review.
- Do not automate scholarships, refunds, cancellations, transfers, credits, or other financial-policy decisions.
- Full LMS authoring and Course Builder behavior are deferred.
- Unresolved items in Samantha's checklist block real-family activation, not the sanitized owner walkthrough.
