# Home School Haven Outcomes and Beta Evidence

## Approved outcomes

- Families quickly understand program fit and choose guidance or direct enrollment (`OUT-FAM-001`).
- Parents manage multiple children through one family account (`OUT-FAM-002`).
- Enrolled families see trusted enrollment, schedule, announcement, and resource information (`OUT-FAM-003`).
- Educators understand assigned programs, schedules, rosters, communications, and resources (`OUT-EDU-001`, `OUT-EDU-002`).
- Administrators centrally manage accurate programs, schedules, educators, families, enrollment, and participation (`OUT-ADM-001`, `OUT-ADM-002`).
- Home School Haven expands responsibly without losing trust, support, or community quality (`OUT-BIZ-001`, `OUT-BIZ-002`).

## Foundation evidence

The beta succeeds when Samantha can demonstrate `SIG-BETA-001` through `SIG-BETA-008`: public understanding and program fit; guidance/direct-registration paths; family-account and student-profile management; administrator operations; assigned educator access; announcement/resource publishing; cross-surface consistency; and structured owner feedback.

These are qualitative owner-review signals, not market validation or product-market fit. Evidence must come from the walkthrough, acceptance checks, and a classified feedback log.
