# Home School Haven — Foundation Review Walkthrough

For Samantha Dodson and invited reviewers.

This is a private preview of the Home School Haven platform. Everything you see uses **sample families and sample children**. No real family's information is in it. Nothing you do here charges anyone, enrolls anyone, or sends anything to a real family.

Your job in this review is to look, try things, and tell us what is right, what is wrong, and what is missing. You can record that feedback inside the platform (step 8).

---

## 1. Opening the preview

You will receive a link from Josh. Open it in a normal browser window.

- The first time the link opens, the address may change once. That is expected.
- **If you see a Vercel page asking for an email or a verification code, stop.** That page belongs to the hosting service, not to Home School Haven, and it means the link has expired. Send Josh a note and he will send a fresh link. Do not enter the sample email addresses there.
- After the link has opened once, the preview stays open in that browser.

## 3. Suggested order

Each step matches one of the eight things this review is meant to show. Take as long as you like on any of them.

1. **Public website** (signed out): home, programs, a program page, calendar, about, resources, contact. Check that names, dates, prices, and descriptions match what you expect.
2. **Asking for help** (signed out): from a program page or the contact page, request guidance, a visit, or discounted-class assistance. Assistance requests stay private.
3. **A parent's view** (Parent, family set up): the family dashboard, children, schedule, announcements, and resources. Start a registration for a child.
4. **Checkout handoff** (same parent): after registering, the page offers "Continue to Secure Checkout", which opens the program's real GoDaddy checkout page. **Please do not complete a payment.** Returning from checkout does not mark anything paid. The registration stays pending until an administrator confirms payment.
5. **Running programs** (Administrator): create or edit a program, review enrollments, move a sample enrollment to confirmed after "verifying" payment, and look at the history each change leaves.
6. **An educator's view** (Educator): the educator sees only assigned programs and only the information they are allowed to see for the children in them.
7. **Announcements and resources** (Administrator or Educator): publish an announcement or a resource, then sign in as the parent to see it.
8. **Your feedback** (Administrator → Reports → Beta review): record what you saw for each item above. Mark each note as a must-fix beta defect, a launch requirement, a next idea, a later idea, or a rejected change. Marking a note does not change the product by itself; agreed changes go through the normal approval process.

## 4. What is intentionally not real yet

- **Sample data only.** Children, families, and contacts are made up.
- **Payment.** Leaving for checkout is not payment. Only an administrator marks a payment as confirmed, after checking it.
- **STEP UP.** No coupon instructions appear. The GoDaddy checkout has no coupon field, so we need to know where families enter it (see 5.6).
- **Policy documents.** The waiver, Code of Conduct, and Parent Handbook appear as sample drafts with no wording. Nothing will be written for you; the real text must come from you.
- **Email.** Sign-in and notification email is not connected to the Home School Haven domain yet.

## 5. Decisions we need from you

These came up while building. None is decided for you.

1. **Two ways to enroll.** A family can enroll from a program page (one program, fewer questions) or through the full family registration (several children and programs, with contacts, health answers, and documents). Should the short path stay, be limited, or be removed?
2. **Stay & Play Sensory Day.** It has a live checkout on your website but is not one of the approved programs. Should it be a program?
3. **Crochet schedule.** The classes page says Fridays, November 6–27, 2–4 PM. Earlier information said Mondays in November. Which is right?
4. **Gardening price.** The checkout shows "Weekly fee $35". Is that the published price?
5. **Tutoring.** It has no online checkout. Is that intended, with families calling instead?
6. **STEP UP coupons.** Where do families enter the STEP UP coupon, and how is a coupon-reduced payment confirmed?

## 6. Before real families can use it

Real families cannot be invited until you confirm the items in the **Policy Confirmation Checklist** (`mps/SAMANTHA-POLICY-CONFIRMATION-CHECKLIST.md`). It covers:

- consent and enrollment terms;
- which child information is truly needed;
- how long records are kept and how they are deleted;
- the waiver, Code of Conduct, and Handbook wording;
- scholarships, refunds, cancellations, and transfers;
- who on your team gets which access.

You can work through it during this review. It does not need to wait until the walkthrough is finished.
