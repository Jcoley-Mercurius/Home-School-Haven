# MPS Discovery & Outcomes Standard v1.0

## Discovery

Establish only what materially shapes the product:

- product context and decision owner
- target geography/platform/business stage
- users and affected parties
- problem evidence and current alternatives
- constraints, obligations, assumptions, and dependencies
- strategic intent and explicit non-goals

Distinguish fact, evidence, assumption, proposal, and approval.

Do not invent a brand name, numeric target segment, market size, pilot size, baseline, or target without user direction or evidence. Preserve such ideas as explicit hypotheses.

## Outcome model

Define:

- **Product purpose:** why the product should exist.
- **User outcome:** meaningful change for a user.
- **Business outcome:** meaningful change for the organization.
- **Output:** something shipped or operated.
- **Guardrail:** result that must not worsen while pursuing an outcome.

Do not represent an output as an outcome. “Launch a dashboard” is an output; “reduce time to understand business performance” is an outcome.

## Outcome record

Track stable ID, statement, beneficiary, evidence, current baseline when known, target/direction, timeframe, measurement source, guardrails, assumptions, owner, status, and linked requirements.

Do not invent numeric targets without evidence or approval. A directional metric may remain proposed until a baseline exists.

## Value proposition

Connect target user, problem/context, desired change, product approach, meaningful differentiation, and evidence. Avoid slogans that cannot guide product decisions.
